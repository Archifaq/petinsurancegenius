Embrace cost research should start with the knobs Embrace itself exposes, not with a borrowed average price from another pet or state. In its public plan materials, Embrace describes adjustable annual limits, annual deductibles, and reimbursement rates as the main levers that change the policy structure and price.

## What Embrace Lets You Adjust

Embrace describes its dog and cat accident-and-illness plans around three core settings: annual limit, deductible, and reimbursement rate. The important cost question is not only which monthly premium appears first in the quote flow; it is what you are giving up or taking on when you move those settings.

A higher deductible can lower the premium but increases the amount you need to absorb before reimbursement starts. A lower annual limit may make a plan cheaper, but it can matter if the same policy year includes surgery, emergency care, diagnostics, or follow-up visits.

## Annual Deductible, Not Per-Condition

Embrace explains that its deductible is annual. That means the deductible is met once per policy term for that pet, rather than separately for every new illness or injury. This is one of the clearest mechanics to compare against carriers that use a per-condition or per-incident structure.

For owners comparing Embrace with another provider, the cleaner question is: "Would I rather manage one deductible per policy year, or a deductible tied to each condition?" The answer can change depending on whether the pet has one ongoing issue or several unrelated claims in the same year.

## Waiting Periods Still Matter

Cost is not useful if the policy timing does not match the pet's situation. Embrace says illness waiting periods begin after the policy effective date, and orthopedic waiting periods for dogs vary by state. A dog owner researching cost before a suspected joint problem should read those timing rules before treating the cheapest quote as the best quote.

## How To Read an Embrace Quote

Use the quote as a policy comparison document, not a scorecard. Check whether exam fees, prescriptions, dental illness, rehabilitation, or complementary care are included in the exact policy version you are considering. Then compare that same structure against another carrier, using the same pet, ZIP code, deductible, reimbursement rate, and annual limit where possible.

This page does not publish an Embrace price because premiums depend on the pet, location, and selected policy terms. The useful work is narrowing which settings you want before you leave for the live quote flow.
