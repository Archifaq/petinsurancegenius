Healthy Paws and Embrace are worth comparing through policy timing first, especially for dog owners thinking about orthopedic conditions. A lower monthly quote is not enough if the waiting-period language does not fit the reason you are shopping.

## Start With Waiting Periods

Healthy Paws publishes detailed waiting-period language that treats hip dysplasia differently from many ordinary accident-and-illness questions. Its public FAQ describes a general waiting-period structure and separate hip dysplasia timing, with state-specific variations for some states.

Embrace also publishes waiting-period guidance, including an illness waiting period and state-specific orthopedic waiting-period rules for dogs. That makes the comparison less about which carrier sounds broader and more about which policy becomes active for the risk you actually care about.

## Then Compare Deductible Shape

Embrace describes its deductible as annual: once met for that pet in the policy term, the same annual deductible is not met again until renewal. Healthy Paws materials focus the shopper on the selected annual deductible, reimbursement level, and either unlimited reimbursement or annual-limit choices, depending on the policy option available.

For a dog with possible hip or ligament risk, write down the same scenario for both providers: enrollment date, first symptom date, exam date, diagnosis date, and treatment date. Then read how each policy handles the timeline.

## What This Comparison Can and Cannot Decide

This page does not rank Healthy Paws above Embrace or Embrace above Healthy Paws. It gives dog owners a safer order of operations: timing language first, deductible and limit structure second, exclusions third, and only then monthly price.
